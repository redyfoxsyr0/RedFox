PHILOSOPHY

RedFox is not a random punishment machine. It is a defense doctrine built on observation, validation, recovery, scoring, and enforcement. Every exploit attempt becomes intelligence, every bypass becomes a new detection, and every attack becomes an opportunity to strengthen the system. We continuously push the boundaries of server-side protection to safeguard legitimate players and stay ahead of evolving threats. We call that Hacker Payback.

Understand the exploit before destroying it.

How RedFox Works

Server-Side Sampling
RedFox continuously monitors player position, velocity, acceleration, humanoid state, grounding, angular velocity, seat state, and movement timing. Every movement decision is verified from the server rather than trusting information provided by the client.
Fly & Hover Detection
RedFox tracks how long a player remains airborne and compares it against valid Roblox movement states such as jumping, climbing, swimming, falling, or vehicle usage. Unsupported hovering, artificial lift, and abnormal vertical movement can generate evidence for fly exploitation.
Noclip Detection
Multiple raycasts, collision checks, and movement validation tests are used to determine whether a player passed through solid geometry. RedFox compares previous positions against current positions and checks whether walls or protected objects were crossed in impossible ways.
Teleport & ClickTP Detection
Distance travelled between server updates is analyzed. Large position jumps without valid physics, vehicles, launch mechanics, or approved movement systems can be identified as teleport behaviour. Movement history is used to determine whether the transition was physically possible.
Velocity & Fling Protection
RedFox watches for abnormal linear velocity, angular velocity, and sudden physics spikes. When impossible movement occurs, the system can restore the player to a previously verified safe position rather than allowing the fling to continue affecting gameplay.
Workspace Integrity Protection
Protected objects can be monitored for deletion, movement, scaling, transparency changes, collision edits, anchoring edits, or other forms of tampering. Optional restoration systems can return protected objects to their original state when modifications are detected.
Interaction Validation
Interactive objects such as ClickDetectors can be validated against player distance and legitimate interaction rules. Impossible interaction attempts can be flagged and investigated as suspicious behaviour.
Grace Handling & False Positive Reduction
Lag spikes, respawns, server ownership changes, recoveries, and temporary replication irregularities are given grace periods. This helps separate legitimate Roblox behaviour from actual exploit activity.
Evidence Accumulation
RedFox rarely relies on a single sample. Multiple suspicious events can be accumulated over time to build confidence before enforcement actions occur. This reduces accidental punishment from isolated anomalies.
Safe Recovery System
When possible, players are restored to previously verified safe locations. Recovery logic helps protect legitimate players from physics bugs, map issues, accidental launches, or temporary replication failures.
Strike Escalation System
Suspicious activity increases a player's risk profile. Repeated violations can escalate through warnings, corrective actions, temporary restrictions, temporary bans, and permanent bans depending on severity and confidence.
Continuous Improvement
Every detection can be refined over time. Thresholds, validation methods, recovery logic, and confidence scoring are designed to evolve as new exploit techniques and edge cases are discovered.

Full Detection Coverage

Movement: Fly, hover, float, speed spoofing, teleport, ClickTP, WASD snap movement, CFrame fly, physics-state fly, fast climb, infinite jump, and jump power spoofing.
Physics Abuse: Fling, walk fling, velocity spikes, angular velocity abuse, body mover fly, VFly while sitting, and player spoofing behavior.
Desync: Head desync, body/root desync, anchored character part abuse, PlatformStand abuse, and suspicious replication states.
Noclip: Multi-height raycasts, blockcasts, inside-wall checks, floor-ignore logic, and persistence thresholds to catch wall phasing.
Workspace Integrity: Protected part deletion, movement, size changes, transparency edits, CanCollide changes, Anchored changes, name changes, and descendant removal.
Remote / Interaction Abuse: FireClickDetector abuse, impossible ClickDetector distance, suspicious remote actions, and interaction spam patterns.
Recovery / Ban Layer: Void abuse recovery, freefall recovery, safe position sampling, strike decay, temp bans, permanent bans, and DataStore ban persistence.
