CORE DOCTRINE

Understand the exploit before destroying it.

RedFox treats anti-cheat like an investigation engine. It observes behavior, validates impossible movement, corrects unstable states, scores repeated evidence, and only then escalates enforcement. The goal is not random punishment. The goal is undeniable proof.

Behavior Intelligence

RedFox does not chase one-frame ghosts. It watches patterns, repetition, timing, and physics impossibilities.

Recovery Before Punishment

Invalid states are corrected first when possible, reducing false positives and keeping real players safe.
