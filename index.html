Philosophy

Effective anti-cheat systems are not measured by how many players they ban, but by how effectively they prevent unfair advantages from impacting legitimate gameplay. RedFox focuses on proactive detection, intelligent recovery systems, and behavioral analysis to stop exploit abuse before it becomes a problem.

I believe the key to patching exploits is understanding them first. Every detection inside RedFox is built from researching how exploit methods work, reproducing them in controlled test environments, and designing protections around their behavior rather than relying on simple signatures.

RedFox operates through multiple layers of validation working together in real time. Movement analysis monitors player positioning, velocity, acceleration, jump states, collision behavior, and humanoid states to identify impossible movement patterns.

Rather than relying on a single detection method, RedFox combines behavioral analysis, collision verification, raycasting, blockcasting, safe-position tracking, workspace monitoring, and server-side validation to build confidence before action is taken.

When suspicious behavior is detected, RedFox attempts corrective actions before punishment whenever possible. Recovery systems restore players to known safe positions, recover characters from invalid physics states, and protect players from unintended fling or void scenarios.
