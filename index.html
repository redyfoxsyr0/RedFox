PHILOSOPHY

RedFox is not built around fear. It is built around proof, validation, correction, and enforcement.

Developer Profile
Anti-Cheat Philosophy

Effective anti-cheat systems are not measured by how many players they ban, but by how effectively they prevent unfair advantages from reaching legitimate gameplay.

RedFox focuses on layered server-side validation. Movement analysis, collision checks, safe-position tracking, blockcasts, workspace integrity monitoring, and exploit behavior scoring work together before punishment is issued.

The goal is not just to remove exploiters. The goal is to make exploiting unreliable, visible, risky, and not worth using.

Behavior-Based Detection

Detects impossible movement and suspicious replication patterns instead of relying only on simple signatures.

Recovery Before Punishment

Corrects invalid states, recovers players from physics failures, and reduces false positives.

Visible Enforcement

Severe offenders can be removed with visible punishment effects that warn everyone watching.

DETECTION MATRIX

A threat grid showing RedFox systems and the exploit behavior they are designed to suppress.

01 Fly Detection

Detects unsupported airborne movement, hover states, float abuse, and fake physics.

02 CFrame Fly

Finds repeated position changes that bypass normal humanoid movement.

03 Noclip

Uses raycast and blockcast checks to catch movement through solid geometry.

04 ClickTP

Flags sudden position jumps, snap movement, and impossible displacement.

05 Fling Abuse

Detects extreme velocity spikes and angular velocity manipulation.

06 Workspace Guard

Monitors protected parts for deletion, movement, size edits, collision edits, and transparency changes.

07 Remote Abuse

Detects suspicious interaction spam and invalid ClickDetector usage.

08 Strike System

Scores evidence before escalating to warnings, corrections, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent bans.
